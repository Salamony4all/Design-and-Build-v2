## 12. Refinement Defect Ledger

**Refinement iteration executed**: 1 (permitted by R1.3 §8)

**Trigger**: Visual inspection identified that wall-parallel pairs with `UNDIRECTED_ANGLE_DEG` in the 4°–5° band were being classified as frame lines when they were actually part of continuous wall faces.

**Affected IDs**: 47 candidates across walls `fr6`, `fr22`, `fr23`, `fr27`, `fr28`

**Defect category**: False jamb pair — parallel lines with near-zero spacing classified as frame when they were part of a continuous wall face.

**Regression test added**: `tests/automatic-detector-clean.test.mjs` — "near-parallel wall face is not a frame pair"

**Correction**: Added a maximum-spacing guard in `generateCandidatesForWall`: pairs with spacing < `PARALLEL_SPACING_MIN_PX` (2.0) or > `PARALLEL_SPACING_MAX_PX` (50.0) are rejected. Also added `FRAME_LINE_MIN_LENGTH_PX` check (3.0) to filter sub-frame-length strokes.

**Before/after categories**:
- Before: retain=373, review=11664, reject=59
- After: retain=326, review=11711, reject=59
- Net change: −47 RETAIN, +47 REVIEW
